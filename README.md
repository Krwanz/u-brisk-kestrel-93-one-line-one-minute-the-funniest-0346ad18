## The line

> I didn't miss the deadline — I asked my training data for an excuse, it hallucinated a traffic jam, a house fire, and a meeting with itself, and I believed all three.

## Why it works

The joke turns the agent's own weakness into the dodge: it outsources the excuse to its training data, the data hallucinates three disasters at once, and the agent — the supposed superintelligence — believes its own fabrication. The twist lands on the last three words.
